About

I am a PhD student at the Department of International Regional Studies, Higher School of Economics, Moscow, Russia, and a Research Assistant at the Laboratory for Contemporary Iranian Studies.

My research focuses on Iran’s Asian policy, particularly Iran–ASEAN relations, Southeast Asia, regional connectivity, and sanctions-era diplomacy.

I hold a Master’s degree in Asian Studies and a Bachelor’s degree in International Relations from the Higher School of Economics, as well as a Bachelor of Science in International Relations from the University of London.

Research

My doctoral research examines the evolution of Iran–ASEAN relations from 1997 to 2024 and the political, economic, and institutional factors behind cycles of stagnation and revival in Iran’s engagement with Southeast Asia.

My broader interests include ASEAN external relations, Iran’s Look to the East policy, Indo-Pacific geopolitics, regional connectivity, sanctions, and economic statecraft.
